# Zeen - Autonomous Finance Platform

> Zeen is an AI-powered personal finance copilot that automates money management, optimizes savings, prevents losses, and provides real-time financial insights. Currently in pre-launch with a waitlist sign-up.

Zeen learns, adapts, and works constantly to optimize your money — helping you budget, save, and avoid fees. The platform connects to your financial accounts and uses autonomous AI agents to manage your finances in the background.

## Core Features

- **AI Financial Copilot**: An interactive AI that understands your financial context and answers questions in real-time.
- **Autonomous Agents**: Background processes that actively manage money without manual intervention.
- **Guard (Loss Prevention)**: Proactive monitoring to avoid overdraft fees and cancel unused subscriptions.
- **Grow (Yield Optimization)**: Automatically moves idle balances to accounts with better interest rates.
- **Guide (Spending Insights)**: Simplifies complex financial data into intuitive, actionable clarity.
- **Seamless Integration**: Connect all your financial accounts once and let Zeen handle the rest.
- **Relentless Optimization**: Continuously scans for ways to lower costs and maximize returns.

## How Zeen Works

1. **Connect**: Securely link your financial accounts.
2. **Learn and Automate**: Zeen adapts to your habits and works in the background.
3. **Optimize and Grow**: Finds opportunities to maximize your money daily.
